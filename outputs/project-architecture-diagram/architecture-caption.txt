This architecture shows an AI interview assessment platform where candidates, recruiters, and administrators use a React/Vite single-page application. The browser handles live camera and microphone capture, MediaPipe-based face, pose, and hand tracking, speech recognition, interview metrics, snapshots, and report visualization. The frontend communicates with a FastAPI backend for authentication, profile management, job advertisements, applications, interview sessions, answer evaluation, and report storage. The backend uses Gemini for AI question generation, question polishing, and answer scoring, while MongoDB stores users, recruiter links, applications, interviews, reports, advertisements, and hidden sessions. External services such as EmailJS, MediaPipe model/CDN assets, and PDF export support the user-facing workflow.
